01About

I trained as an electrical engineer at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University, then moved into machine learning research at KFUPM in Saudi Arabia. There I worked on small-object detection for maritime search and rescue and on detecting malicious network traffic.

What I care about most is making models that hold up outside the lab: on noisy real-world images, on shifted data, and on limited hardware. I'm especially interested in deploying AI in resource-constrained environments, and I plan to continue into doctoral research.

Before research, I spent about a year and a half in industry in data roles. That taught me to start from the business question and to explain results to people who don't read code.

02Research & projects

Computer vision

ALT-YOLO: altitude-conditioned small object detection

Detecting people and boats in drone footage for maritime search and rescue. I evaluated YOLO26-P2 with maritime-tuned anchors on SeaDronesSee ODv2, and proposed altitude-conditioned SAHI inference and glare-aware HSV/CLAHE preprocessing for small targets in open water.

PyTorch · YOLO · SAHI · KFUPM, 2026

Network security · Co-authored research paper

Detecting QUIC-based command-and-control traffic

An ML pipeline that extracts flow features from packet captures with PyShark and benchmarks eight classifiers at spotting command-and-control traffic hidden in encrypted QUIC connections.

Python · PyShark · scikit-learn · KFUPM, 2026

Medical imaging

LUNA-Analysis: lung nodule classification and localization

A reproducible pipeline on the LUNA dataset that classifies nodules as benign or malignant and localizes them as a secondary task. It comes with documented preprocessing, notebooks, source code and tests.

Python · PyTorch · UAB, 2026

Federated learning · In progress

Robust federated learning for lung nodule analysis

Testing how well Byzantine-robust aggregation (Krum, Trimmed Mean) protects federated training on LUNA16 against poisoning attacks such as label flipping and sign flipping, with hospitals simulated as non-IID clients.

Python · PyTorch · UAB, 2026
