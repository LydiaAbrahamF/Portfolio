02Research & projects

Computer vision · UAV imagery

ALT-YOLO: altitude-conditioned inference for maritime search and rescue

Detecting people and boats in drone footage. I evaluated YOLO26-P2 with maritime-tuned anchors on SeaDronesSee ODv2, and proposed altitude-conditioned SAHI inference and glare-aware HSV/CLAHE preprocessing. A per-altitude evaluation protocol showed a 23.5-point mAP@50 gap between medium- and high-altitude images, pointing to scale variation as the main bottleneck.

PyTorch · YOLO · SAHI · OpenCV · KFUPM, 2026

Statistical fingerprinting for QUIC-based C2 detection

A four-VM testbed with two Merlin C2 configurations and a background-noise class, with flow fingerprints extracted via PyShark and strict time-based splits. Random Forest and XGBoost reach 94.7–94.8% accuracy on 4 classes. Noise-aware training cuts false-positive C2 alerts from 42% to under 3% while keeping recall at 96.6–97.8%.

Python · PyShark · XGBoost · with Shahad Alghamdi · KFUPM, 2026

ML methodology

Reassessing ensemble learning in software defect prediction

A leakage-safe, nested cross-validation study comparing tree-based ensembles (RF, GB, XGBoost, CatBoost) with non-tree stacking across 10 public datasets. Tree-based stacking with a logistic-regression meta-learner gave the best MCC and PR-AUC, confirmed with Wilcoxon and Friedman/Nemenyi tests.

scikit-learn · XGBoost · CatBoost · with Haya Alsharef, Tania Sheila · KFUPM, 2026
